Why the old IPK worked (ath9k)

Core loop in Tim’s waterfall-update. Mesh disconnect was expected and warned. UI polls forever; each sample is one full-band hop.

#waterfall-update step
1spectral_count = 32
2ath9k: write chanscan · ath10k: background then trigger
3Thinned channel freq list from get_rfchannels (step bw/10)
4iw <iface> scan freq <list> passive (RF hop + activity)
5disable, then read entire spectral_scan0
6Parse TLVs → full-band buckets (max / 95% / 50%)
7Child process always disables on exit
ath9k reliability factors
  • chanscan is the ath9k debugfs mode designed for spectral-during-scan
  • Passive freq-list scan produces many HT20 FFT samples across the band in one shot
  • Absolute dBm conversion (noise + rssi + 10*log10(bin²/sum)) made SNR bars meaningful
  • Percentile stacking hid single-sample noise

Why AC often failed

Do not copy Tim’s ath10k primary path. Tim’s ath10k branch still depended on iw scan while spectral was enabled. On QCA988x IBSS/mesh that often returns an empty spectral_scan0 or wedges the radio if ctl is left enabled / scan runs too long. Keep our isolated worker, timeout+recover, and survey fallback. Gaps that hurt “stable and working”

GapDetail
No chanscan path ALL uses multi-section IBSS retune + background/trigger. Fewer samples than Tim’s scan-driven FFT; retune failure → survey (misleading).
Bins are raw magnitudes parseFftTlvs / samplesToSweep skip max_exp, rssi, and 10*log10. Scale inconsistent across chipsets (water_scale was 50 vs 15 in Tim’s UI).
ath9k one-shot is current-channel only background+trigger without hop is fine for dwell, weak for “what’s on the band”.
ath10k iw scan never enabled from ucode allowScan=false is correct for mesh; empty FFT → survey is expected, not a Tim-style full FFT band map.

Chosen approach

ModeDecision
Current / single Keep mesh-safe background+trigger (ath9k inline; ath10k isolated). Do not make iw scan the default.
ath9k ALL / wide stitch Add classic Tim path — chanscan + thinned iw … scan freq … passive + full relay read + disable — as primary (ALL already accepts RF disruption).
ath10k Keep isolated short pulses + survey. Do not promote Tim’s scan-while-spectral as primary.
Signal math Port Tim’s absolute-dBm conversion into parseFftTlvs; keep honest placeBinsOnBand (no stretch).
